Lasagna

Lasagna is a Italian Traditional kind of pasta, its prepared with sheets of pasta interspersed

with the ingredients of your preference, but generally its made with bolognese sauce or bechamel sauce

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 500g pound ground beef
  • 500g pound bulk Italian sausage
  • 4 (425g) cans tomato sauce
  • 1 (450g) can sliced mushrooms, drained
  • 1 teaspoon garlic salt
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • ½ teaspoon dried thyme
  • ¼ teaspoon dried basil
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 (425g) container ricotta cheese
  • 3 large eggs, beaten
  • ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 500g shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175 degrees Celsius
  2. Cook ground beef and sausage in a large pot over medium heat
  3. Stir in tomato sauce, mushrooms, garlic salt, oregano, thyme, and basil
  4. Season with salt and black pepper. Simmer 30 minutes
  5. Meanwhile, combine ricotta cheese, eggs, and Parmesan cheese in a bowl
  6. Cover bottom of a 22x33 cm baking dish with a thin layer of meat sauce; layer 3 lasagna noodles over top
  7. Spread about 1/3 ricotta cheese mixture over noodles
  8. Sprinkle about 1/4 mozzarella cheese over top
  9. Spoon about 1/3 meat sauce over mozzarella cheese
  10. Repeat layering twice more, finishing with remaining mozzarella cheese.
  11. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese melts and sauce bubbles, about 90 minutes
  12. Cover lasagna with foil if cheese begins to get too dark
  13. Let sit before serving for 10 to 15 minutes
  14. Enjoy!
