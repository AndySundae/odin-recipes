Spaghetti Pesto

Spaghetti Pesto is a Italian Traditional Dish, made with aldente pasta with Pesto Sauce

Made with mainly basil, garlic, olive oil and nuts

Ingredients

  • 2 Cloves - Garlic
  • 15g - Parmesan Cheese, Freshly Grated
  • 15g - Pinenuts, Untoasted * See note down below
  • 45g (1 Bunch) - Basil Leaves
  • 3 Tbs - Extra Virgin Olive Oil
  • 1 1/2 Tbs - Sea Salt (1/2 Tbs for pesto, 1 Tbs for pasta water)
  • 1/4 tsp - Ground Black Pepper
  • 250g - Spaghetti or Pasta of your choice
  • Parmesan Cheese and Basil to serve

Steps

  1. Place a sauce pan with 3/4 full of water to the heat and bring it to boiling
  2. Meanwhile ad 2 garlic cloves to a blender with 15g of Parmesan Cheese an 15g of peanuts
  3. Blend all together, add 1 branch (45g) of basil
  4. Add 3 spoons of olive oil, 1 tsp of salt and 1/4 tsp of pepper, and blend until all is mixed
  5. Throw salt to the boiling pot, when its boiling add the Spaghetti and wait 11 minutes
  6. Then we will have a perfect al-dente Spaghetti, scoop a cup of the cooking water
  7. drain the pasta, get a big bowl and mix the pesto with the Spaghetti
  8. Add the cup of water we separated
  9. And ready!
  10. Enjoy!
