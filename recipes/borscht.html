Borscht Soup

Borscht is a Ukrainian Traditional Soup, with the main ingredient being beetroots,

it can have extra stuff like meet, potatoes, cabbage, etc...

Ingredients

  • Broth (vegetable, beef, or chicken) - 2 liters
  • Diced potatoes - 4-5 units
  • Shredded cabbage - 1/4 of a medium cabbage or 1/2 of a small one
  • Grated beets - 2-3 units
  • Grated carrots - 1-2 units
  • Chopped onions - 1-2 units
  • Tomato sauce or peeled and diced fresh tomatoes: 2-3 tablespoons or 1-2 tomatoes.

Steps

  1. Cook the meet, carrots and beets together on abundant water
  2. Once ready move the meet, carrots and beets to another plate
  3. Boil again the residual water
  4. while waiting for the water to boil, stir-fry the onions and grate the beet and carrots
  5. When the water boils, incorporate the potatoes
  6. Once the onions look a bit transparent, add the grated beet and the grated carrots
  7. Add the cabbage to the boiling water for about 10 minutes
  8. Add dill, pepper, salt to your taste and bay leaf
  9. Add the stir-fry to the boiling water and wait for all to cook
  10. When its al ready, Enjoy!
